2. Summary

  • We only process the data the app needs: your account, your profile, your gear, your races and checklists.
  • There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics tools.
  • Your data is stored on servers in Frankfurt am Main, Germany.
  • Only if you have a race filled in automatically is the content of the race page or the text you pasted sent to an AI service for analysis (see section 6).
  • You can delete your account with all its data in the app at any time.

3. Account and sign-in

To use the app, you create an account with an email address and a password. The password is only stored in encrypted form (as a hash). We use your email address for signing in and for emails concerning your account (e.g. confirming or resetting your password). So that these emails reach you in your language, we store the language of the app (German or English) with your account.

Sign in with Apple: Alternatively, you can sign in with your Apple ID (“Continue with Apple”). Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland) then confirms your identity and sends us a user identifier, your email address and, on first sign-in and if you choose, your name. With Apple you can choose to hide your email address; we then receive an anonymous relay address from Apple. We store the name as the display name in your profile. No password is created with this sign-in method. So that we can revoke your Apple sign-in with Apple when you delete your account, we store an access token issued by Apple on our server for this purpose; it is deleted together with your account.

The sign-in emails (confirming your email address, resetting your password, notice of a password change) are created by the Supabase authentication service (see section 9) and sent via the email service Resend (Resend, Inc., USA; sent via servers in Ireland, EU). This involves processing your email address, the content of the email and technical delivery data (e.g. time, delivery status). We have a data processing agreement with Resend (Art. 28 GDPR); where data is transferred to the USA, this is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing your account).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app under the user agreement).

4. Your content in the app

We store what you create in the app: gear items (name, category, weight), your races and distances, races you create privately together with their gear list, and your checklists (assignments and check-offs). This data is visible only to you.

In your profile, you can voluntarily add a display name and links to your public runner profiles on ITRA (itra.run) and UTMB (utmb.world). We only store the link itself and do not retrieve any data from these sites. If you tap the link, the respective site opens in your browser; the privacy policy of the respective provider applies there. The statistics in your profile (e.g. races completed, kilometers, season overview) are calculated only from your own races in the app.

The shared race catalog contains only information published by the organizers (races, distances, mandatory gear) and no personal data. Races you create yourself are not added to the catalog.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

5. Reporting errors in the race catalog

Using “Report an Issue” in the packing list, you can tell us about errors or changes concerning a race from the catalog. For this, we store your report (type of error, affected race or item, your text), the time and your account, in order to limit misuse (at most 10 reports per day) and to be able to match the report if we have questions. For every report, we receive an email in our support inbox (sent via Resend, see section 3) containing the report and your email address, so that we can reply if we have questions. We use the reports only to correct the catalog. Please do not include any personal information in the text.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (a feature you use) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in an accurate catalog and in protection against misuse).

6. Filling in races automatically (AI analysis)

When you use “Read Link” or “Paste Text”, the following happens:

  • The link or the pasted text is sent to our server. For a link, the server loads the public race page.
  • So that pages that only load their content in the browser can also be read, the server additionally has the public race page rendered by the Jina Reader service of Jina AI GmbH, Berlin, Germany. Only the link to the race page is transmitted, no data about you.
  • The page content or your text is sent for analysis to Anthropic PBC, 548 Market Street, PMB 90375, San Francisco, CA 94104, USA. Anthropic returns a structured gear list. If the gear list is on a linked subpage, Anthropic may retrieve further public pages of the organizer for this purpose.
  • No account data is sent to Anthropic (no email address, no user identifier). Please do not paste any personal information into “Paste Text”.
  • Under Anthropic’s terms, content submitted via the API is not used to train AI models.
  • The transfer to the USA is based on appropriate safeguards under Art. 46 GDPR (EU Standard Contractual Clauses) or on an adequacy decision, insofar as the provider is certified.

To prevent misuse of this feature, we store the time and the type (link or text) of each analysis with your account and limit the number (currently 5 per day and 20 in 30 days).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (a feature you requested); for the limit, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in protection against misuse).

7. Purchases and subscriptions (BibReady Pro)

You buy BibReady Pro via the App Store. Payment is handled exclusively by Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland); we do not receive any payment or card data or any information about your Apple ID. The app checks with Apple on your iPhone whether there is a valid subscription (term, type of subscription) in order to unlock the Pro features. This information is not stored on our servers. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the purchase. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the features purchased).

8. Data on your device (offline mode)

So that you can use your packing list without an internet connection, the app additionally stores your races, packing lists and gear on your device. Changes you make offline are synced as soon as a connection is available again. This cache is deleted when you sign out. The app also remembers on the device whether it has already offered you the starter gear.

If you use “Add to Reminders” for the shopping list, the app asks once for access to your reminders and creates a list there. The entries are stored by Apple on your device or in your iCloud; we do not receive any data from your reminders.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG) (storage strictly necessary for the feature).

Weather Check

For the Weather Check (BibReady Pro), the app uses the start coordinates stored in the catalog for races from the catalog. For races you create yourself, it determines the coordinates from the location you entered using Apple’s map service; it also uses this service to determine the time zone of the start location. With the coordinates and the race date, the app requests weather data from the Apple Weather service (WeatherKit) of Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland. Only the race location and the date are transmitted, not your location and no account data; according to Apple, it does not use the requests to identify you. The results are stored on your device for a few hours. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the feature).

9. Hosting and servers

The app uses Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA) for sign-in, database and server functions. The data is stored in a data center in Frankfurt am Main (EU). We have a data processing agreement with Supabase (Art. 28 GDPR). When the server is accessed, technically necessary data such as IP address and time is processed in server logs to ensure operation and security (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Website bibready.app

This website (bibready.app with legal notice, privacy policy, terms of use and support) is provided via GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as IP address, time and the page visited in server logs in order to deliver the pages and protect them against attacks. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The website does not set cookies and does not use any analytics or tracking tools. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (secure and reliable provision of the website).

10. Retention and deletion

We store your data for as long as you have your account. You can delete your account at any time under Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes all associated data: profile, gear, races, checklists, races created privately, your error reports and the log of AI analyses.

11. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Just write to us at the email address above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your place of residence.

12. Changes

If the app or the legal requirements change, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.

In case of any discrepancy, the German version prevails.
