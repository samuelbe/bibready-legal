2. What the app does

BibReady helps you keep track of the mandatory gear for races: you can choose races from a shared catalog or create your own races, record your gear, match it against the mandatory gear list and check off your packing list. Optionally, the app can automatically read the information from a race page or a pasted text using AI.

The basic features are free. Additional features are available with the optional BibReady Pro subscription (see section 3).

The information in the app does not replace the official race regulations. Organizers can change their mandatory gear at any time, for example at short notice due to the weather. Lists read automatically by AI may contain errors or be incomplete. Therefore, check the organizer’s current list before every race; the app links to the official source for each race. You are responsible for the consequences of missing or incorrect gear (e.g. time penalties, disqualification) and for your own safety on the course.
