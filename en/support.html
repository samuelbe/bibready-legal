Support

Help with BibReady – the app for mandatory gear at endurance races

Contact

Do you have a question, found a bug or have an idea? Write to us at support@bibready.app. We can help fastest if you briefly describe what you did and include your iPhone model and the app version (Profile → Settings, at the very bottom).

Frequently asked questions

How do I add my race?

In “Races”, tap + and search the catalog. If your race is listed, you only need to choose your distance. If it is missing, create it yourself under “Create New Race”: manually, from a template or automatically via a link or pasted text.

My race takes place every year. Do I have to add it again?

Yes. Each edition of a race is a separate entry in the catalog with its own date and its own mandatory gear list, because the list can change from year to year. Your race from the previous year stays under “Past” together with your packing list.

How often can I have a race filled in automatically?

Reading a race automatically via link or text uses AI and is therefore limited: currently 5 times per day and 20 times in 30 days. If BibReady recognizes the link as a race from the catalog, it does not count. Once the limit is reached, you can always create the race manually.

The mandatory gear list in the catalog is out of date. What should I do?

Open the race’s packing list, scroll all the way down and tap Report an Issue. We check the race regulations and correct the catalog. Until then, the organizer’s official list always applies; you can reach it via the link in the packing list.

Why are my items not matched correctly?

BibReady suggests a matching item from your gear based on its name. If the suggestion does not fit, tap the entry in the packing list and choose the right item or “Don’t Have It”.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Your races, packing lists and gear are stored on your iPhone once you have opened the app with an internet connection. Offline, you can check off and assign items; the changes are synced as soon as you are back online. Creating new races and editing gear only works online.

I did not receive a confirmation email.

Check your spam folder. On the sign-in screen, you can have the email sent again. Open the link on the iPhone on which the app is installed.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password?. You will receive an email with a link; open it on your iPhone and set a new password in the app.

How do I delete my account?

In the app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes all your data. If that does not work, send us an email from your account’s email address.

How much does the app cost?

BibReady can be used for free: the catalog and all mandatory gear lists, your gear and up to three upcoming races with a complete packing list. With the optional BibReady Pro subscription, you get an unlimited number of upcoming races plus extras such as the Weather Check, PDF export and race comparison. You manage and cancel the subscription in the iPhone Settings under your name → Subscriptions, or in the app under Profile → Settings.
